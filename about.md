## Our Mission

Associazione Atypical is committed to **creating an inclusive and supportive community** for individuals with diverse needs and perspectives. We believe that when people come together with mutual respect and shared purpose, extraordinary things can happen. We foster environments where diverse voices are heard, valued, and celebrated, working toward meaningful change and lasting positive impact in our communities.

## Our History

**Founded in May 2018**, Associazione Atypical was established on the belief that **diversity is our greatest strength**. With 8 founding members, we have grown into a thriving community of passionate individuals working together to create positive change.

### Key Milestones:
- **May 2018** - Official founding with 8 founding members committed to inclusive community building
- **2020-2022** - Expansion of activities and growth of our member base
- **2021** - Affiliation with SBN Napoli Pole, joining the library network and establishing the **Angela Lembo Library** as our operational hub
- **Ongoing** - Continuous expansion of cultural events, workshops, and community initiatives

Our journey has been marked by:
- **Building Connections** - Creating spaces where people from all walks of life can meet and collaborate
- **Supporting Growth** - Providing resources, mentorship, and opportunities for development through workshops and skill-sharing
- **Creating Impact** - Launching projects and initiatives that make a real difference in our community
- **Fostering Inclusivity** - Ensuring that everyone has a voice and feels valued, regardless of background or abilities
- **Library Stewardship** - Operating the Angela Lembo Library as a community hub (Open: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 16:00-19:00)
