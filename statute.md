## Article 1: Name and Purpose

**Associazione Atypical** is a non-profit **cultural and recreational association** (Associazione Ricreativa Culturale) founded on **May 2018** and officially registered in **Minori, Salerno (SA), Italy**. We are dedicated to creating inclusive communities and empowering individuals with diverse backgrounds and perspectives.

### 1.1 Legal Classification
- **Type:** Non-profit recreational and cultural association (non-recognized)
- **Headquarters:** Via Vescovado n.2, Minori (SA) 84010, Italy
- **Duration:** Unlimited
- **Purpose:** Cultural and recreational activities serving the community

### 1.2 Mission
The association aims to:
- Foster inclusive and supportive communities for all individuals
- Empower individuals to reach their full potential
- Promote diversity, equity, and belonging
- Create platforms for collaboration, learning, and growth
- Provide cultural and educational opportunities
- Operate the Angela Lembo Library as a community hub

### 1.3 Core Values
Our organization operates on the principles of:
- **Inclusivity** - Welcoming all individuals regardless of background, abilities, or perspectives
- **Community** - Believing in collective support and shared strength
- **Transparency** - Operating with honesty, openness, and accountability
- **Empowerment** - Supporting individual growth and development
- **Growth** - Fostering continuous learning for all members
- **Impact** - Creating lasting positive change in our communities

---

## Article 2: Membership

### 2.1 Who Can Join
Membership is open to any individual who:
- Believes in our mission and values
- Agrees to abide by this statute
- Contributes positively to the community

### 2.2 Rights and Responsibilities
**Members have the right to:**
- Participate in association activities and events
- Vote on important decisions (during general assemblies)
- Propose new initiatives and projects
- Access resources and support

**Members are responsible for:**
- Respecting all community members
- Following our code of conduct
- Contributing to the community's growth
- Maintaining inclusive practices

### 2.3 Member Categories
- **Active Members** - Regular participants and contributors
- **Supporting Members** - Those offering financial or material support
- **Honorary Members** - Recognized for exceptional contributions

---

## Article 3: Governance Structure

### 3.1 Leadership
The association is led by:
- **Board of Directors** - Elected leadership team overseeing operations
- **General Assembly** - All members participate in major decisions
- **Working Groups** - Teams focused on specific projects or areas

### 3.2 Decision-Making
Major decisions are made democratically:
- **General Assemblies** - Held regularly for community input
- **Consensus-Building** - Seeking agreement on important matters
- **Voting** - When consensus cannot be reached
- **Transparency** - All decisions documented and communicated

### 3.3 Roles and Responsibilities
- **President** - Overall leadership and representation
- **Vice President** - Supporting leadership and operations
- **Secretary** - Documentation and communications
- **Treasurer** - Financial management and reporting
- **Project Coordinators** - Leading specific initiatives

---

## Article 4: Code of Conduct

All members commit to:

### 4.1 Respect and Inclusivity
- Treating all people with dignity and respect
- Respecting diverse backgrounds and perspectives
- Creating safe and welcoming spaces

### 4.2 Professional Conduct
- Communicating respectfully
- Acting with integrity
- Avoiding discrimination and harassment
- Resolving conflicts constructively

### 4.3 Confidentiality
- Protecting sensitive member information
- Using shared data responsibly
- Respecting privacy and personal boundaries

### 4.4 Commitment to Values
- Supporting the association's mission
- Contributing constructively to projects
- Promoting inclusivity in all interactions

---

## Article 5: Meetings and Assemblies

### 5.1 General Assemblies
- Held at least **twice per year**
- All members invited to attend
- Decisions require quorum (minimum 50% attendance)

### 5.2 Board Meetings
- Held monthly to oversee operations
- Open to member observation upon request
- Minutes documented and shared

### 5.3 Quorum Requirements
- General Assembly: 50% of active members
- Board Meetings: 2/3 of board members
- Working Groups: As determined by team

---

## Article 6: Financial Management

### 6.1 Funding Sources
The association may receive funding through:
- Member contributions
- Grants and sponsorships
- Fundraising events
- Donations

### 6.2 Financial Transparency
- Annual financial reports published
- Quarterly updates provided to members
- All funds used to advance our mission
- No private profit distribution

### 6.3 Budget Approval
- Budget approved annually by General Assembly
- Significant expenditures require board approval
- Regular financial audits conducted

---

## Article 7: Amendments and Changes

### 7.1 Statute Amendments
- Proposed by members or board
- Discussed in General Assembly
- Require 2/3 majority vote to pass
- Communicated to all members

### 7.2 Review
This statute is reviewed:
- Annually for clarity and updates
- When significant changes are proposed
- To ensure continued alignment with our mission

---

## Article 8: Dissolution

Should the association ever dissolve:
- All assets distributed to advance similar missions
- Member contributions returned fairly
- Community impact preserved and celebrated

---
