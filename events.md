## Want to Host an Event?

Have an idea for an event that would benefit our community? We'd love to hear about it!

### Steps to Propose an Event:
1. **Share Your Idea** - Tell us what you'd like to host
2. **Collaborate** - Work with our team to plan the details
3. **Execute** - We'll support you in bringing it to life
4. **Celebrate** - Enjoy the event with your community

## Event Guidelines

To ensure a welcoming and safe environment for all, we ask that attendees:
- **Be Respectful** - Treat all members with dignity and kindness
- **Participate Inclusively** - Welcome people from all backgrounds
- **Follow Our Code of Conduct** - Maintain professional and respectful behavior
- **Arrive On Time** - Help us start and end events as scheduled
- **Provide Feedback** - Share your thoughts so we can improve
